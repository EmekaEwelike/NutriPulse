# Step-by-Step Guide: Google Play Store & Apple App Store Developer Accounts

This comprehensive guide outlines the exact process, cost, prerequisites, and steps required to register developer accounts for publishing Android apps on the **Google Play Store** and iOS apps on the **Apple App Store**.

---

## 1. Choosing the Right Account Type

Both Apple and Google require you to choose between two primary developer account types during enrollment:

| Account Property | Individual / Personal Account | Organization / Company Account |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Target Audience** | Solo developers, freelancers, hobbyists | Incorporated businesses, LLCs, corporations, non-profits |
| **App Store Seller Name** | Your personal legal name | Your registered legal business name |
| **D-U-N-S Number Required?** | No | **Yes** (Free 9-digit business identifier) |
| **Team Management** | Limited/No granular developer access | Full multi-user team role assignment |
| **Verification Requirements** | Personal Government Photo ID | D-U-N-S Number, Legal Entity proof, Phone Verification |

> **What is a D-U-N-S Number?**  
> A D-U-N-S (Data Universal Numbering System) number is a unique 9-digit identifier issued by Dun & Bradstreet. It is mandatory for **Organization** accounts to prove business legitimacy. Obtaining a D-U-N-S number is **100% free** via [Dun & Bradstreet](https://www.dnb.com/), though assignment may take 2–7 business days.

## 2. Google Play Console Setup (Android)

* **Registration Fee:** **$25 USD** (One-time fee)
* **Annual Renewal:** None ($0 recurring)
* **Estimated Approval Time:** 24 hours to 3 business days

### Prerequisites
* A dedicated Google Account (e.g., `developer@yourdomain.com`).
* Credit or Debit Card capable of international transactions.
* Government-issued Photo ID (Passport, Driver’s License, or National ID).
* D-U-N-S Number & official business registration documents (Organization accounts only).

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Navigate to the Console:** Visit [play.google.com/console/signup](https://play.google.com/console/signup).
2. **Sign In:** Log in with your dedicated developer Google Account.
3. **Select Account Type:** Choose **Personal** or **Organization**.
4. **Developer Profile Details:** Fill in your public developer name, contact email address, and phone number.
5. **Pay Registration Fee:** Pay the **$25 USD** one-time registration fee.
6. **Complete Identity Verification:** 
   * Submit a clear photo of your government ID.
   * Verify your phone number and email address.

> **CRITICAL: 14-Day Testing Requirement for Personal Accounts**  
> Google mandates that all new **Personal** developer accounts run a closed test with a minimum of **12 to 20 opted-in testers for at least 14 consecutive days** before requesting production access to publish apps publicly. **Organization** accounts are exempt from this requirement.

## 3. Apple Developer Program Setup (iOS)

* **Registration Fee:** **$99 USD / year** (Annual recurring membership fee)
* **Annual Renewal:** $99/year (Fee waiver available for eligible non-profits and educational entities)
* **Estimated Approval Time:** 24–48 hours (Individual); 1–2 weeks (Organization)

### Prerequisites
* An Apple Account (Apple ID) with **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)** enabled.
* Government-issued Photo ID.
* D-U-N-S Number, legal entity status, and a public corporate website (Organization accounts only).

### Step-by-Step Instructions
1. **Enrollment Method:**
   * **Option A (Recommended):** Download the **Apple Developer** app from the iOS/Mac App Store. (Fastest enrollment & identity verification process).
   * **Option B:** Visit the [Apple Developer Program Enrollment Web Page](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/).
2. **Sign In:** Log in using your Apple Account with 2FA enabled.
3. **Choose Account Type:** Select **Individual** or **Organization**.
4. **Identity & Entity Verification:**
   * **Individual:** Enter legal name and address, then verify identity with photo ID.
   * **Organization:** Enter business legal name, D-U-N-S number, headquarters phone number, and website URL. Apple will call the designated contact to confirm legal authority.
5. **Payment:** Complete payment of the **$99/year** enrollment fee.

## 4. Pre-Launch Checklist

- [ ] **Dedicated Emails:** Set up clean, professional email addresses specifically for developer communications.
- [ ] **Exact Name Matching:** Ensure legal names on registration forms **match government IDs or legal incorporation filings exactly**.
- [ ] **Free D-U-N-S Application:** Apply early on Dun & Bradstreet if choosing an Organization account. Beware of third-party scam sites charging fees for D-U-N-S numbers.
- [ ] **Payment Card Ready:** Have a credit card ready that accepts USD payments and recurring subscriptions.
- [ ] **Device for 2FA:** Keep an iOS/Mac device or mobile phone ready for Two-Factor Authentication prompts.
