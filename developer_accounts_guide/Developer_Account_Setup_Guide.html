Developer Account Setup Guide

Google Play Store (Android) & Apple App Store (iOS)

1. Account Type Selection

Before registering on either platform, choose between an Individual or Organization account based on your setup:

Property Individual / Personal Organization / Company
Target Audience Solo developers, freelancers, hobbyists LLCs, Corporations, Non-profits
App Store Seller Name Your personal legal name Your official legal business name
D-U-N-S Number Needed? No Yes (Free 9-digit business ID)
Team Roles & Permissions Limited / Single user Full multi-user team role management
ℹ️ What is a D-U-N-S Number?
A D-U-N-S (Data Universal Numbering System) number is a unique 9-digit business identifier provided by Dun & Bradstreet. It is mandatory for Organization accounts. You can obtain it 100% free from Dun & Bradstreet (takes 2–7 business days).

2. Google Play Console Setup (Android)

Fee: $25 USD One-time Renewal: $0

Prerequisites

  • A dedicated Google Account (e.g., developer@yourcompany.com).
  • Credit or Debit Card accepting USD payments.
  • Government-issued Photo ID (Passport, Driver's License).
  • D-U-N-S Number & corporate registration files (Organization only).

Registration Steps

  1. Go to play.google.com/console/signup.
  2. Sign in with your dedicated developer account.
  3. Choose Personal or Organization.
  4. Enter developer profile information (Developer Name, Email, Phone).
  5. Pay the $25 USD one-time registration fee.
  6. Complete identity verification by uploading your ID and verifying phone/email.
⚠️ Important Testing Rule for Personal Accounts
Google requires new Personal developer accounts to conduct a closed test with at least 12 to 20 testers for 14 consecutive days before public app publishing is unlocked. Organization accounts are exempt.

3. Apple Developer Program Setup (iOS)

Fee: $99 USD / year Renewal: Annual

Prerequisites

  • Apple Account (Apple ID) with Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) enabled.
  • Government-issued Photo ID.
  • D-U-N-S Number, legal entity status, and a working website (Organization only).

Registration Steps

  1. Download the Apple Developer app on iOS/Mac (Recommended for fastest approval) OR visit developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/.
  2. Sign in with your Apple Account (2FA required).
  3. Select account entity type (Individual or Organization).
  4. Complete identity verification (Photo ID for individuals; D-U-N-S + legal authority phone verification for organizations).
  5. Pay the $99 USD/year enrollment fee.

4. Pre-Registration Checklist

  • ✔️ Dedicated developer email accounts created.
  • ✔️ All details match government ID / legal documents exactly.
  • ✔️ Applied for free D-U-N-S number (if registering an Organization).
  • ✔️ International payment card ready.
